Privacy Policy.

Last updated: 12 November 2026.

1. Who is responsible

The data controller is Artificial Media LLC, 30 N Gould St #23749, Sheridan, WY 82801, USA. Contact: kristian@artificialmediastudio.com.

2. What this policy covers

This policy covers this website, the project brief form, FUNSWAP and ART.ME ACADEMY. Production and website projects themselves are contracted separately, by individual offer and invoice.

3. What we collect and why

  • Orders: your email address and what you bought, to deliver the product and keep required records.
  • FUNSWAP photos and video: your three photos, the trend you chose and the finished video, only to make and deliver your video.
  • ART.ME ACADEMY: the name you'll use in the academy, your email address, your payment details from Stripe (amount, date and payment reference, not your card number), your membership and the work you submit for grading, to run the academy.
  • Project briefs: your name, email address, company (if you give it), what you need, your timing, your budget and anything you write in the form, to reply to you and prepare an offer.
  • Messages: what you send us by email or tell us by phone, to answer you.

Card details are entered with Stripe and are not seen or stored by us.

We process this data to perform our contract with you, to meet legal obligations, and, for replying to enquiries, on the basis of our legitimate interest in answering you. We process project briefs to take steps at your request before a contract, and on the basis of our legitimate interest in answering enquiries.

4. Photos you upload

  • Purpose: only to make the FUNSWAP video you ordered.
  • Legal basis: performance of our contract with you, and your consent.
  • Who processes them: Higgsfield, the AI service that generates your video, and Cloudflare, which stores your photos in the European Union until they are deleted. Your photos are sent to Higgsfield automatically, right after payment.
  • How long: photos and the finished video are deleted automatically 30 days after delivery. If you leave without paying, your photos are deleted within the hour. According to Higgsfield, the photos it receives are deleted within seven days. You can ask us to delete them earlier at kristian@artificialmediastudio.com.
  • What we don't do: we do not use your photos to train AI models or for marketing, and we do not publish them, without your separate consent.

5. How long we keep it

FUNSWAP photos and videos are deleted 30 days after delivery, or earlier, if you ask us. Other data is kept for as long as it is needed for the purpose above or as the law requires for accounting records.

Project briefs are kept for 12 months after we receive them, unless they lead to a contract. You can ask us to delete yours earlier.

If you start a reservation and don't pay, your name and email are deleted within two hours. We keep your ART.ME ACADEMY reservation details for as long as you hold a seat or are a member. After a refund, or after you leave, we delete them within 12 months, except payment records we must keep under tax and accounting law.

6. Who processes it for us

  • Stripe, for payments.
  • GitHub Pages, which hosts this website and processes technical request data such as your IP address.
  • Google Fonts, from which this website loads its fonts, so Google receives your IP address.
  • Resend, which sends our emails, such as FUNSWAP order and delivery emails and confirmations of project briefs. For that purpose Resend receives your email address and the content of the email.
  • Northwest Registered Agent, which hosts our mailbox, for emails you send us and our replies.
  • Higgsfield, the AI service that generates your FUNSWAP video, which receives your photos for that purpose.
  • Cloudflare, which receives the project brief form, stores briefs and ART.ME ACADEMY reservations and protects the form against automated abuse (Turnstile), and stores FUNSWAP photos and videos in the European Union. For that purpose Cloudflare processes technical data such as your IP address and browser details. For Turnstile, Cloudflare's Turnstile Privacy Addendum also applies.

Some of these providers are in the United States, and Artificial Media LLC is a US company, so your data may be transferred outside the European Union.

7. Cookies and analytics

This website uses no analytics and no marketing cookies. The project brief form saves your answers in your browser so you can finish later. They stay on your device, are deleted when you send the brief, and are removed after 7 days otherwise. FUNSWAP keeps your order reference and small previews of your photos in your browser while you order. They stay on your device and are gone when you close the tab.

8. Your rights

You can ask us for access to your personal data, and for its correction, deletion, restriction or transfer, and you can object to its processing. Write to kristian@artificialmediastudio.com; we reply within 3 business days. If you are in the European Union, you can also complain to your data protection authority.
