CLIENT FINDER

The hard part was never the video. It was finding who needs one.

CLIENT FINDER is the tool that does that part. It is how we find our own clients. In our studio it runs as a set of skills and analyses; in the academy you get it as one tool. You unlock it when you pass BASIC and enter CREATOR.

1 · FINDS
  • new businesses that already earn revenue and have no video, in industries where their competitors do
  • briefs on freelance marketplaces asking for a video or motion design for a product or a logo
  • startups that have raised enough to pay for an ad, but not enough to have their own team
  • products that sell well on online marketplaces and have no ad
  • restaurants and hospitality businesses without video
2 · RANKS

It picks the most promising ones, by industry, platform, social network and sales channel.

3 · DRAFTS

For each one it writes a business offer based on an analysis of that client.

CLIENT FINDER finds them. We help you with the first one. You make the work.
