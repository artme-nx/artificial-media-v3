Refund & Cancellation.

Last updated: 10 November 2026.

This policy covers FUNSWAP and ART.ME ACADEMY, sold by Artificial Media LLC.

Production and website projects are not covered here. They are contracted separately, by individual offer and invoice.

1. FUNSWAP

FUNSWAP is a personal video made to order. You get a full refund only if the video can't be made, or if we refuse your order.

2. ART.ME ACADEMY

Reservation. The €50 incl. VAT reservation is refunded in full until 18 April 2027, with no conditions and no questions. Write to kristian@artificialmediastudio.com.

Monthly billing. Monthly billing starts on 18 April 2027 only after you confirm it when the academy opens. If you don't confirm, you are not charged.

Cancelling after 18 April 2027. You can cancel online at any time, through the link in your subscription emails, without calling us. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the month you have paid for. There is no refund for part of a month.

Coming back. If you cancel and take a refund, and come back before 18 March 2027 while a seat is available, your price is €50 a month incl. VAT. If you cancel after the academy opens and come back later, the public price of €250 a month incl. VAT applies.

What you pay others. Your subscriptions and software from other providers (Claude, Higgsfield, Adobe Premiere Pro, Adobe After Effects, cloud storage) are paid to those providers and are not refunded by us.

Age. Members must be 18 or older, or have a parent or legal guardian make the purchase.

3. Consumers in the European Union

EU law gives consumers 14 days to withdraw from a purchase. For FUNSWAP, you expressly request immediate delivery and acknowledge that you lose that right once the video is delivered. You confirm this with a checkbox before you pay. For the ART.ME ACADEMY reservation, the refund period above runs longer than 14 days.

4. Problems and disputes

Before opening a dispute with your bank, please contact kristian@artificialmediastudio.com. We reply within 3 business days.
